# v1.3 Enhanced — Picto Box fixed, fast forward, .wua archives, crash reports from the app, smoother 60 fps

An update to the Enhanced fork of the Android port of The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker HD. It
brings in upstream Android port v0.6 and v0.7 and a set of fixes from the original ZeldaWWHDRecomp
project.

**The APK contains no game files, no game code and no keys.** It installs over v1.0, v1.1 or v1.2
and keeps your saves and settings. After updating, the first start compiles the game code on your
device again (a few minutes, once).

## Highlights

- **Picto Box pictures are no longer black.** The pictures the game saves and shows in the Picto
  Box now hold the actual picture.
- **Fast forward cutscenes and dialogues (new).** *Options → Mods → Fast forward cutscenes /
  dialogues*: hold ZR (R2) during a cutscene or a conversation and it plays up to 4× as fast, as
  fast as your device can draw it. Every frame is still played, so nothing is skipped; text boxes
  still wait for your button. Each one is switched on separately.
- **Cemu `.wua` archives as a game source (new).** Put your `.wua` file in the folder you choose
  and the app extracts the game from it, with no keys needed.
- **Crash reports from the app.** After a crash, the next start offers the log in Android's share
  menu; *About* shares the latest logs. They now include the GPU, driver, settings and the game
  function that crashed, and game halts write a log too. No personal data.
- **Bloom strength (new).** *Options → Graphics → Bloom strength*: from Off to 200%; 100% is the
  original game.

## Smoother 60 fps (from the original project)

- **Flags and sails no longer turn into stretched polygons** in the in-between frames (the Rito
  flags on Dragon Roost Island).
- **No more one-frame jumps of carried and attached things**: items in Link's hand, a carried bomb,
  the boat's sail and yard.
- **Night-sky stars stay put** while the camera turns, instead of jittering against the sky.
- **Menus and screen fades run at their normal speed**, and the pause menu always closes.

## Fixes

- **Medli missing from Dragon Roost Island or the Earth Temple** after using the sword cheat: the
  cheat now equips the Master Sword and Mirror Shield without changing your story progress. A save
  already affected can be repaired on a PC with `tools/savegame/wwsave.py repair-medli`.
- **Crash in Tingle's jail on Windfall with quick doors** (*Mods → Quick doors*).
- **Random black shadows for a whole session** on some launches: the lighting buffer could end
  up with a level that was never drawn.
- **A fine grid around everything on the ground at internal resolutions above 1x**: the game's blur
  of shadows, ambient occlusion and bloom now covers the same area as on the console.
- **Shader inputs the vertex shader doesn't write** now get the value the console's GPU gives them,
  instead of undefined values.
- **Copies of depth surfaces at different resolution** no longer use a GPU operation Adreno doesn't
  support for those formats.

## Installing

Install the APK over v1.0, v1.1 or v1.2. New installs: see the v1.0 notes (disc image, disc key
and `common.key` in one folder, then *Extract from your disc image…*), or choose a folder with your
`.wua` archive.

Requirements: Android 11 or newer, 64-bit ARM, Vulkan 1.1, about 2 GB of free storage and memory.
40 fps needs a screen with a 120 Hz mode (the option only shows there).

If the app crashes, share the log it offers at the next start (or from *About*) with your report.
