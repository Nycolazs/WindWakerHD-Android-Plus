# v1.3.1 Enhanced — Performance at high resolutions restored

A fix for the frame rate drop some of you saw in v1.3 at internal resolutions above 1x (for example
60 → 54 fps on an Adreno 830 and 30 → 21 fps on an Adreno 725 at 3x).

**The APK contains no game files, no game code and no keys.** It installs over any earlier version
and keeps your saves and settings.

## Fixes

- **Frame rate drop at 2x and 3x resolution.** v1.3 made the game's blur of shadows, ambient
  occlusion and bloom match the console at higher resolutions, which removes a fine grid around
  everything standing on the ground. It read up to 9 times more texels at 3x, and that cost a lot of
  GPU time. It is now **off by default**, so performance is back to v1.2's.

## New setting

- **Console-accurate blur** (*Options → Graphics*, off by default): turns that blur back on, if your
  device has GPU time to spare at your resolution. It now takes 4 reads per tap at 2x to 4x instead of
  up to 16, so it costs much less than in v1.3. It applies at once; at 1x it changes nothing.

Everything else is as in v1.3: see the v1.3 notes.

## Installing

Install the APK over v1.0, v1.1, v1.2 or v1.3. Requirements: Android 11 or newer, 64-bit ARM,
Vulkan 1.1, about 2 GB of free storage and memory.

If the app crashes, share the log it offers at the next start (or from *About*) with your report.
